About

Someone who has sat on both sides of the tournament desk

I have spent six years inside Bangladesh's PUBG Mobile scene. As a team manager I looked after rosters, schedules and tournament requirements. As a project manager I planned the events those rosters played in.

I have managed four teams since 2019. Inertia Esports (INES) was Bangladesh's first team to represent the country at the regional level in PMCO and PMPL South Asia, and Wolves Den Esports won PMBC 2020.

On the event side I have run online and LAN tournaments of up to 780+ teams and led networking for LAN events involving 3,000+ teams. I also hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering.
